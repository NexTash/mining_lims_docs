# The process step by step

This section follows one request from the first phone call to the approved report. Each
page says **who** acts, **what** they do in the software and **what happens automatically**.
The statuses below are exactly the ones you see on the forms and on the workspace counters.

## Request

| Status | Meaning | Who moves it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Technician has registered the request; the price is still the list price. | Head of Laboratory: *Set Final Price* |
| **Pending** | Price set, invoice created and e-mailed; waiting for payment. | Head of Laboratory: *Paid* |
| **Ready** | Payment confirmed; samples are created and wait to be received. | - |

## Sample

| Status | Meaning | Who moves it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Expected at the laboratory. | Technician: *Accept* or *Reject Sample* |
| **Sample Rejected** | Failed inspection at receipt (note + photo); customer e-mailed. | Technician: *Re-deliver* when the replacement arrives |
| **Received** | Accepted and registered; label and receipt can be printed. | Technician: *Split into Pieces*, *Mark Prepared* or *Preparation Failed* |
| **Preparation Failed** | Could not be prepared (note + photo); Head of Laboratory notified. | Technician: *Receive Replacement* |
| **Prepared** | Specimen ready; data sheet created. | Engineer: complete the data sheet |
| **Tested** | Data sheet completed; results calculated; reports available. | Head of Laboratory: *Approve* or *Reject* |
| **Test Rejected** | Sent back by the Head of Laboratory with a note; data sheet open again. | Engineer: complete the data sheet again |
| **Reviewed** | Approved by the Head of Laboratory, customer report chosen. | Director of Centre: *Approve* or *Reject* |
| **Review Rejected** | Sent back by the Director with a note. | Head of Laboratory: *Regenerate Reports* or *Return to Engineer* |
| **Approved** | Final. Chosen report e-mailed to the customer and shown in the portal. | - |

## Who is told what

The system sends an e-mail at every hand-over: the customer receives the invoice, a
rejection notice and the approved report; staff receive a notice when work is waiting for
them. The full list is on [E-mail notifications](../notifications.md).
