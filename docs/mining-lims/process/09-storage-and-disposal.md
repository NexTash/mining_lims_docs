# 9. Storage and disposal

**Who:** Technician.

Tested material is kept for a retention period in case the customer has questions, then
returned or disposed of. The **Storage & Custody** tab of the sample records this.

## Storing

1. Choose the **Storage Location** (rack, shelf, room; see
   [Storage Location](../modules/storage-location.md)) and set **Stored On**.
2. **Retention Days** is filled from the Test (or the laboratory default in Settings) and
   can be changed per sample. **Discard Due** is calculated as the receipt date plus the
   retention days.

## When the retention period ends

The Technician receives an e-mail the day after **Discard Due**. Open the sample, choose
the **Discard Method** (Disposed, Returned to Customer, Retained for longer), add **Discard
Remarks** and save. The date and your name are recorded.

## Custody log

Every hand-over is listed in the **Custody Log**: receipt from the customer, preparation,
splitting into pieces, preparation failure, storage. The log is written by the system and
cannot be edited or deleted by anyone. The **Chain of Custody** print shows the log with
the signatures.
