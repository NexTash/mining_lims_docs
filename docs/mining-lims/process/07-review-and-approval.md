# 7. Review and approval

Two people check every result before it leaves the laboratory: the **Head of Laboratory**
reviews the test, the **Director of Centre** gives the final approval.

## Review by the Head of Laboratory

**Sample Tested → Reviewed** (or **Test Rejected**).

1. Open the **Tested** sample (workspace counter or the e-mail). On the **Testing & Review**
   tab add the **Photo Before / After Testing**, the **Failure Mode** and any
   **Laboratory Comments** that should appear on the report, if the engineer has not.
2. Look at the results and the data sheet (**View > Observation Data Sheets**) and the
   reports: **Reports > Internal Report** shows everything including the validity checks,
   **Detailed Report** adds the readings and charts to the results, **Brief Report** shows
   results only.
3. Choose the **Report for Customer**: **Brief** (default) or **Detailed**. This is the
   report the customer will receive by e-mail and in the portal.
4. Optionally untick a specification under **Standards Applied** if its limits should not
   be printed for this sample.
5. **Actions > Approve.** The sample becomes **Reviewed**, your name and the time are
   recorded, and the Director of Centre is notified.

If something is wrong, write a **Review Note** (mandatory) saying what must be corrected and
use **Actions > Reject**. The sample becomes **Test Rejected**, the data sheet opens again
and the engineer receives your note by e-mail. After the correction the engineer completes
the sheet again and the sample comes back as **Tested**.

## Approval by the Director of Centre

**Sample Reviewed → Approved** (or **Review Rejected**).

1. Open the **Reviewed** sample (counter or e-mail) and check the chosen report:
   **Reports > Detailed Report** or **Brief Report**.
2. **Actions > Approve.** The sample is **Approved**; your signature and the approval date
   go on the report; the customer receives the chosen report as a PDF by e-mail and can
   download it from the portal. A parent sample is approved automatically when all its
   pieces are approved.

To send it back, write a **Director Note** (mandatory) and use **Actions > Reject**. The
sample becomes **Review Rejected** and the Head of Laboratory is notified. The Head then
either corrects report settings and uses **Actions > Regenerate Reports** (back to Tested
for a new review) or **Actions > Return to Engineer** (back to Test Rejected).

!!! info "Signatures"
    Reports carry three signatures: the engineer who completed the data sheet, the Head of
    Laboratory who reviewed, and the Director who approved. They come from each user's
    profile; see [Users and signatures](../modules/users-and-signatures.md).
