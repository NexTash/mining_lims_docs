# 8. Reports and the customer portal

## The three reports

Each Test has three report templates. All of them are print formats: open **Reports** on
the sample, choose the report and print or save it as PDF from the print preview.

| Report | Content | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal** | Specimen and test information, results, every reading table, charts, validity checks, comments, signatures. | Laboratory staff, audits. |
| **Detailed** | Results with uncertainty, reading tables, charts, photos, comments, signatures. | Customers who need the full picture (chosen by the Head of Laboratory at review). |
| **Brief** | Specimen information, results with uncertainty, comments, signatures. | Customers by default. |

Every report shows the test method standard and edition, the specimen and borehole
details, the testing machine, the report limitation text and the laboratory letterhead
from [Settings](../modules/settings.md). Reports of samples that are not yet approved carry
a **DRAFT** watermark.

Other printable documents: **Sample Label (QR)**, **Sample Receipt Form** and **Chain of
Custody** on the sample, the **Observation Data Sheet** on the sheet, the **Invoice** on
the invoice.

## E-mail to the customer

When the Director approves a sample, the chosen report is attached as a PDF to an e-mail
sent to the request's contact e-mail. The e-mail is listed in the timeline of the sample.
If it must be re-sent, open the sample, click the e-mail icon in the timeline and attach
the report from the print menu.

## The customer portal

Customers who have a portal login (see [Customer](../modules/customer.md)) sign in with
their e-mail address and see the **laboratory portal**:

- their **requests** with status;
- their **invoices** with amount, due date and payment status, each with a **Download PDF**
  link (the same invoice that was e-mailed);
- the **samples** of each request with their current status (Waiting, Received, Tested,
  Approved ...), without laboratory-internal details;
- a **Download report** link for every approved sample, giving the report the Head of
  Laboratory chose for them.

Customers cannot see prices other than on their invoices, cannot see other customers' data
and cannot change anything. There is no public report page; everything requires a login.
