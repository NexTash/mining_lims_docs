# 1. Creating a request

**Who:** Technician (the Head of Laboratory can also do it). **Result:** a Request with
status **New**.

A customer asks for testing by phone, e-mail or in person. The Technician registers it:

1. Open **Requests** and click **+ Add Request**.
2. Choose the **Customer**. This is mandatory. If the customer does not exist yet, ask the
   Head of Laboratory to create it (see [Customer](../modules/customer.md)).
3. Check the **Contact Email**. It is filled from the customer's primary contact and is the
   address that receives the invoice, rejection notices and the approved reports.
4. Fill in **Project / Location** (for example the mine or borehole site) and, if known,
   **Required By**.
5. In **Items**, add one line per test: pick the **Test** and enter how many **Samples**
   will come for it. Add a second line for a second test, and so on.
6. Optionally describe what will be delivered in **Material Summary** (cores, boxes, bags)
   and add **Notes**.
7. **Save**.

The request gets a number (REQ-2026-00012) and the status **New**. The Head of Laboratory is
now expected to set the price; the banner on the form says so.

!!! info "What the Technician does not see"
    Prices are shown only to the Head of Laboratory and the Director of Centre. The
    Technician sees the tests and the sample counts but not the price per sample, the
    total or the invoice.

Field by field: [Request form reference](../modules/request.md).
