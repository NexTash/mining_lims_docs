# Login and navigation

## Logging in

Open the address of your laboratory's Mining LIMS in a browser and sign in with the e-mail
address and password you were given. Laboratory staff land on the **Mining LIMS** workspace.
Customers land on the customer portal instead (see
[Reports and the customer portal](../process/08-reports-and-portal.md)).

To change your password or upload your signature, click your name at the top right and
choose **My Settings**. See [Users and signatures](../modules/users-and-signatures.md).

## The workspace

The home page shows:

- **Quick Access** - shortcuts to Requests, Samples, Observation Data Sheets, Invoices,
  Customers, Tests and Equipment.
- **Requests** and **Samples** - one counter per status (New, Pending, Ready, Waiting,
  Received ...). Click a counter to open the list filtered to that status. This is the
  quickest way to see what is waiting for you.
- **Needs Attention** - rejected samples, failed preparations, open data sheets, pending
  invoices and equipment with calibration due.
- **Reports & Masters** - links to every form and report, grouped by module.

The sidebar on the left has the same groups plus the **Dashboard**, which shows the same
counters with charts over time.

## Lists and forms

Every kind of record (a Request, a Sample, a Test ...) has a **list** and a **form**.

- The **list** shows all records with their status. Use the filters at the top to narrow
  it down (for example Samples with status *Waiting*) and the search box to find a record
  by name or code. Click a row to open it.
- The **form** shows one record. Fields are grouped in sections and, on larger forms such
  as the Sample, in **tabs** along the top (Receiving, Processing, Testing & Review,
  Storage & Custody).
- **Save** (Ctrl+S) stores your changes. Records that go through a workflow are
  **submitted** when the process starts; after that only the fields the process needs stay
  editable.

## The Actions menu and the status banner

Records with a status (Request, Sample, Observation Data Sheet, Contract) change status
through the **Actions** button at the top right of the form. It only offers the actions your
role may take at the current status, for example *Accept* and *Reject Sample* on a Waiting
sample for a Technician.

Under the title of such a form a coloured **banner** tells you where the record stands and
what should happen next. Read it first when you open a record you do not know.

## Other things on a form

- **Print** - the printable documents of the record (Sample Label, Sample Receipt Form,
  Chain of Custody, the three Test Reports, the Invoice). They open in a print preview you
  can print or save as PDF.
- **Attachments and comments** - the panel on the left of a form holds files and comments.
  Photos asked for by the process (receipt, rejection, failure, before and after testing)
  have their own fields on the form.
- **Timeline** - at the bottom of the form, every change, comment and e-mail is listed with
  who did it and when. This is the audit trail of the record.
- **Help** (top bar) and **⋯ > Documentation** - open the matching page of this guide.
