# Reports and dashboard

## Workspace counters

The **Mining LIMS** workspace shows one counter per Request and Sample status, plus
**Needs Attention** counters (rejected samples, failed preparations, draft data sheets,
pending invoices, calibration due). Click a counter to open the filtered list.

## Dashboard

**Dashboard > Mining Laboratory** (sidebar) shows the same counters with charts: samples
and requests by status, samples received and approved per month, invoices by status,
tests by count, engineer workload and equipment by calibration status. Charts can be
filtered by period with the controls on each chart.

## Reports

All reports are under **Reports** in the sidebar. Use the filters at the top, then
**Menu > Export** for Excel.

| Report | What it shows | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sample Register** | Every sample with customer, request, test, specimen code, borehole, dates and status. The laboratory's sample log. | All staff |
| **Equipment Usage** | Which equipment was used on which samples, by whom, for how long. | All staff |
| **Audit Trail** | Every change made to laboratory documents (requests, samples, data sheets ...): who changed what, from which value to which, and when. | Head of Laboratory, Director |
| **Employee KPI** | Per person: samples received, prepared, tested, reviewed and approved in the period, and turnaround times. | Head of Laboratory, Director |

Each list view can also be turned into a quick report with **Report view** (the icon next
to the list title) and saved with your own columns and filters.
