# Users and signatures

**Purpose:** every person using Mining LIMS has a user account with one role that decides
what they can see and do (see [Roles and permissions](../roles-and-permissions.md)).

## Creating a user (System Manager)

1. Open **Users** (search "User" in the top bar), click **+ Add User**, enter e-mail and
   name.
2. On the **Roles** tab tick one of: Technician, Engineer, Head of Laboratory, Director of
   Centre. Customers get their login from the Customer form instead.
3. Save. The person receives a welcome e-mail with a link to set a password.

## Your signature (every user)

Reports are signed with the signatures of the engineer, the Head of Laboratory and the
Director. Each person uploads their own:

1. Click your name at the top right, then **My Settings**.
2. In the **Laboratory Signature** section upload the **Signature** image (a clear scan or
   photo on white background, PNG or JPG) and enter your **Position** as it should be
   printed under the signature.
3. Save.

The Director's printed name on reports can also be set in [Settings](settings.md).
