# FAQ

**The Actions button does not show the action I need.**
Either the record is not at the right status, or your role is not allowed to take that
action. The banner under the title says who acts next. See
[Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

**I cannot reject a sample.**
A rejection needs a **Rejection Note** and a **Rejection Photo** on the Receiving tab (for a
preparation failure: Failure Note and Failure Photo on the Processing tab). Fill them in,
save, then use the action.

**The data sheet cannot be completed.**
Check the red banner: either no result has been calculated yet (a required reading is
missing), a **blocking** validity check fails (see the Checks panel), or the method needs
a data-logger file that has not been imported.

**The data sheet is locked.**
The sample has been reviewed. Ask the Head of Laboratory to reject the test; the sheet
opens again.

**The equipment I used is refused.**
Its calibration has expired or was never recorded. Record the calibration under Equipment,
or ask the Head of Laboratory about the *Block On Expired Calibration* setting.

**The customer did not receive the invoice or the report.**
Check the **Contact Email** on the request and the e-mail in the record's timeline. Use
**E-mail to Customer** on the invoice to re-send it. If no e-mails go out at all, the
server's outgoing e-mail account needs attention (ask the administrator).

**The report shows DRAFT.**
The sample is not Approved yet. The watermark disappears once the Director approves.

**How do I change the price for one customer?**
On the New request, the Head of Laboratory edits **Price per sample** on the item line
before *Set Final Price*. The Test's service price stays the list price.

**How do I add a test the laboratory did not have before?**
See [Adding a new test](modules/test.md#adding-a-new-test). The data sheet definition is
usually done together with NexTash.

**Where do I see who changed what?**
At the bottom of every form (timeline) and in the **Audit Trail** report.
